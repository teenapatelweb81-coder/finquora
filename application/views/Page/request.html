Common FAQs

My payment is done but the account is still not created. What should I do?

This may happen when your amount is held with the payment gateway and yet to be received in the company's account. Do not worry, once the amount is credited to the company's account, your account will be created and you will be informed via mail. Else, the payment gateway will refund your amount to your account as per their rules and regulations.

My refund is yet to be received even after many days. What should I do?

As your amount is on hold with the bank/payment gateway, it will be refunded as per the bank's/payment gateway's rules and regulations.

I misunderstood the company's services/did payment by mistake. Can I get a refund?

The payment of Membership fees is refundable only in accordance with the company's Cancellation & Refund Policy: Click here

I was shown Pre-Approved Loan Offers as per my Eligibility but I didn't get any actual loan. Why is this so?

To gain the exact understanding of what the Pre-Approved Loan Offer/Eligibility is, kindly watch the explanatory video – link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yX6WpAqHv6s

Who can get a GST Return?

Be it Channel Partner or Customer, the person who has updated their GST information in their portal will get the GST Return.

I have changed my mind and I don't wish to avail company's services. Can I get a refund?

The payment of Membership fees is refundable only in accordance with the company's Cancellation & Refund Policy: Click here

For this concern, you're requested to call the company on 9081545972 between 10 AM to 5 PM – Monday to Saturday (only business days) – and explain your issue.

Who can TDS return?

TDS will be given only to the ones whose referral payout has been generated. If your TDS is deducted, you can contact your CA. If your TDS has been deducted and it's not showing, then you can contact the company's customer care number between 10 AM to 5 PM – Monday to Saturday (only business days).
